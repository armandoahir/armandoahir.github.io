About me

Most people my age love the digital world, but I have a more complicated relationship with it. Born in Italy to indian parents in the early 2000s, my life was always surrounded by electronics and the internet. However, over time, I developed a deep resentment toward how we use these tools.

I spent years being addicted to social media, wasting hours watching the "perfect" lives of others just to feel relevant. It is a vicious cycle that ruins our real world experience. As the saying goes, "Give a man a mask and he will be sincere." On the internet, people often use the "fake" anonymity to spread hate instead of being honest. It took me a long time to break out of that bubble and reclaim my personal time.

Personal academic story

My academic path was also a struggle against the system. When i was young, i struggled to build a strong character, and my shyness led to poor performance in middle school. The system decided i wasn't ready for a scientific high school, so i was pushed toward a vocational institute. While i gained good technical skills in electronics, i lacked the theoretical foundation i truly wanted. Even my self-confidence suffered because, as a child, no one taught me the importance of simple things like physical self-care and hygiene.

After my high school diploma, i eventually enrolled in computer science engineering at university. The gap between a vocational school and university was huge, especially in Italy, where the system is obsessed with theory and lacks practical lab hours. I often had to work twice as hard to keep the pace.

Webpage purposes
